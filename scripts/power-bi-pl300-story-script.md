# Power BI / PL-300: story-script rewrite

Your PL-300 reel (`srt1_Original.srt`) rewritten on the structure of the two reference scripts (the thyroid one and the mother one).

---

## 1. The structure both reference scripts follow

| # | Beat | From the reference scripts |
|---|------|------------------|
| 1 | **Open mid-scene with a line of dialogue** | "Every morning at 6 a.m. my phone rings. *Priya, where is the medicine?*" |
| 2 | **A three-count with numbers. The third one breaks the pattern** | "At 40, my mother took 50 mcg. At 60, my grandmother took 100. At 14, I took 200." *(the transcript says 14/16, but it's almost certainly 40/60. That's what makes the twist work.)* |
| 3 | **Flip line: same words with one swapped, so the meaning reverses** | "I can't live **with** my mother because I don't want to live **like** my mother." / "I left home for the hustle. The hustle left me in a hospital." |
| 4 | **Three objects. The last one is the secret** | "a comb full of thin hair, a double XL pad and a purse under my pillow to hide my dirty little secret" |
| 5 | **A one-line truth** | "When a blood report says no to dreaming, she also stops sleeping." |
| 6 | **The turn** | "But tell me, and this is a wild thought…" |
| 7 | **A scene, a pause, then dialogue** | "…but the day she left: *Hello mom, have you reached the airport?*" |
| 8 | **The reveal in three parallel lines. Each one flips an earlier belief** | "She fed the friends she called bad company. She visited the office she told me to quit." |
| 9 | **A short punch that pays off a setup** | "My mother had taken her first flight alone. For me." |
| 10 | **A closing line built from something planted early** | pillow → "they stop getting to your head" · "never took a flight alone" → "her first flight alone" |

**The rules underneath:**

- Nothing is listed. Everything is revealed.
- Every early detail comes back later (6 a.m., the pillow, the flight).
- Sentences stay short and specific: numbers, objects, real lines of dialogue.
- The rule of three is everywhere.

Your original *lists* the tips ("Pehle… Doosre… Teesri…"). In this version each tip is a reveal that flips something you believed.

## 2. Rewritten script (Hinglish, beat by beat)

```text
[1 · HOOK]
"Power BI aata hai? Prove karke dikhao."
Interview mein sir ne bas itna poocha.
Maine full confidence mein kaha —
"Sir, mere paas chaar certificates hain."

[2 · THREE-COUNT]
Chaar certificates.
Chaalis ghante ke videos — 2x speed pe.
Aur ek sawaal, jiska jawab mere paas nahi tha —
"Microsoft ka PL-300 hai?"
"Sir… PL-300? Ye kaun sa certificate hai?"

[3 · FLIP LINE]
Sir ne kaha —
"Udemy, Coursera ke certificates batate hain
ki tumne Power BI DEKHA hai.
PL-300 batata hai ki tumne Power BI SEEKHA hai.
Aur companies ko seekha hua chahiye."

[4 · THREE OBJECTS + SECRET]
Mera Power BI teen jagah rehta tha —
folder mein chaar PDF,
LinkedIn pe chaar "Happy to share" posts,
aur resume pe bold mein "Advanced"…
jiska sach sirf mujhe pata tha.

[5 · ONE-LINE TRUTH]
Jab screen pe "Completed" aata hai na,
hum seekhna bhi complete maan lete hain.

[6 · THE TURN]
Lekin socho — aur ye ajeeb lagega —
agar koi certificate aapko fail kar sake, toh?

[7 · SCENE + PAUSE]
Usi raat maine search kiya — "PL-300 kya hai?"
Aur jo mila…

[8 · REVEAL IN THREE]
Jis padhai ke liye maine chaar courses kiye —
wo Microsoft Learn pe free thi. Paisa sirf exam ka.

Jis exam ke liye laga center jaana padega —
wo ghar se bhi ho jaata hai.
Camera on, mic on… aur 2x speed off.

Aur jis certificate ka mujhe naam tak nahi pata tha —
uska practice test bhi free tha.

[9 · PUNCH]
Maine diya.
Aur zindagi mein pehli baar…
kisi certificate ne mujhe fail kar diya.
Pehli baar, kisi certificate ne sach bola.

[10 · CLOSING]
Toh resume pe "Advanced" likhne se pehle,
ek baar ye test de dena.
Kyunki jo certificate aapko fail nahi kar sakta…
wo kabhi prove nahi kar sakta
ki aapko Power BI aata hai.

[CTA: on screen / caption, or after a 1-second beat]
Topics aur free resources chahiye? Comment kijiye "PL-300".
```

## 4. English version (for captions or an English cut)

```text
"Do you know Power BI? Prove it."
That's all the interviewer asked.
I said, full of confidence —
"Sir, I have four certificates."

Four certificates.
Forty hours of videos — at 2x speed.
And one question I had no answer to —
"Do you have Microsoft's PL-300?"
"Sir… PL-300? Which certificate is that?"

The interviewer said —
"Udemy and Coursera certificates say you've WATCHED Power BI.
PL-300 says you've LEARNED Power BI.
And companies hire 'learned'."

My Power BI lived in three places —
four PDFs in a folder,
four "Happy to share" posts on LinkedIn,
and "Advanced" in bold on my resume…
and only I knew the truth about that one.

When the screen says "Completed",
we assume the learning is complete too.

But think — and this will sound strange —
what if a certificate could fail you?

That night I searched, "What is PL-300?"
And what I found…

The learning I took four courses for —
was free on Microsoft Learn. You only pay for the exam.

The exam I thought needed a test centre —
can be taken from home.
Camera on, mic on… and 2x speed off.

And the certificate I didn't even know the name of —
had a free practice test.

I took it.
And for the first time in my life…
a certificate failed me.
For the first time, a certificate told me the truth.

So before you write "Advanced" on your resume,
take that test once.
Because a certificate that can't fail you…
can never prove you know Power BI.

[CTA] Want the topics and free resources? Comment "PL-300".
```

## 7. Notes

- **Length:** about 265 spoken words, roughly 70 s at your pace (the original SRT is 212 words in 57 s). For about 63 s, drop beats 5 and 6.
- **Placeholders:** "chaalis ghante", "usi raat", the folder and the LinkedIn posts. Swap in your real details so the story stays true.
- **Fact softened:** "bahut saari companies mein mandatory" became "companies ko seekha hua chahiye". Commenters can easily challenge "mandatory".
- **PL-300 status:** still active in 2026. The certification lasts one year, and the yearly renewal is a free online assessment.
- **Delivery:** pause at every "…". Stress DEKHA / SEEKHA. Leave a second of silence after "sach bola" and after the last line. Keep the CTA on screen so the last spoken line is the punch.
